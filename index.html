The gap this fills

Your cleaner is fast. Your guests are silent — until they leave a review.

01

Cleaners work on a schedule

Turnovers are timed tight between checkout and check-in. Small things — a wobbly cabinet, a low toiletry stock — aren't what they're there to catch.

02

Guests don't tell you first

By the time something shows up in a review, it's already cost you a rating — and probably a future booking.

03

You can't always be there

A monthly walkthrough means someone's physically checking your property with fresh eyes, on a schedule, whether you're local or not.

Exactly what you're getting

An inspection and a report. That's the whole job — on purpose.

Included every visit

  • Full walkthrough: cleanliness, inventory, function, safety, cosmetic wear
  • Photo documentation of every flagged item
  • Written report delivered within 48 hours
  • Priority ranking on issues — urgent / soon / cosmetic
  • Visit history so patterns and recurring issues are visible over time

Not part of the base service

  • Cleaning, restocking, or fixing anything myself
  • Guest communication or check-in/checkout handling
  • Hiring or supervising your cleaner or handyman
  • Co-host access to your listing
How it works

Scheduled around your calendar, not your guests.

We find the window

I check your booking calendar for a gap — cleaned, but not yet occupied — and schedule around it, no disruption to guests or cleaners.

The walkthrough

I go room by room against a consistent checklist, photographing anything worth flagging.

Your report

A clear, organized write-up lands in your inbox within 48 hours — ready for you or your team to act on.
